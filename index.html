Your 7TV emotes,
everywhere on Discord.

7DC started as a simple idea: bringing the 7TV Twitch emote experience — especially wide-emotes — to Discord. It's tied to your account and works across DMs, group chats and servers, with no bot needed in each channel.

Install 7DC How it works

Features

Use everywhere

Thanks to Discord's "User Install" feature, your emotes follow you globally.

7TV sync

Pulls directly from your current active 7TV emote-set.

Reactions

React to any message using your emotes — guide below in Commands.

Autocomplete

Search for emotes instantly through the Discord command UI.

How to Use

1

Link your account

Connect your Discord account to your 7TV profile.

2

Add 7DC to Discord

Install the app to your account.

3

Start sending emotes

Use the commands below to send emotes or configure your experience.

Commands

/emote [name] Search and send an emote from your active emote-set (alias: /e).
/size [small/medium/large] Change your default emote preview size (default = small).
/set [emote-set ID or link] Change your active emote-set or fall back to default.
/help Show info about 7DC.
Emote Reactions Guide
1. Open the message context menuRight-click (Desktop) or long-press (Mobile) a message and open Apps → 7DC → React
2. Select your emoteChoose the emote you want to use from your active 7TV emote-set.
3. DoneYour selected 7TV emote will be added as a reaction to the message.

Performance & Caching

  • Instant search. Your 7TV emote-list is cached in-memory for 10 minutes, so suggestions appear instantly without waiting for an API response.
  • Efficient API usage. Caching prevents unnecessary requests to 7TV's servers, keeping the integration smooth and stable.

Data & Privacy

  • No private data. The app does not collect or store passwords, emails or private messages.
  • Public IDs only. It only maps your public Discord ID to your public 7TV ID — both already visible on your public profiles.
  • No tracking. Your emote usage is never tracked, stored or logged. 7DC simply acts as a bridge to display your own 7TV collection.

Limitations

  • Emote previews are not possible.
  • Emote Reactions do not feature autocompletion.

Notes

  • 7DC is not affiliated with 7TV.
  • Emotes remain property of their respective creators on 7TV.
  • Users are responsible for proper use according to Discord guidelines and 7TV rules.
  • This project follows Discord Developer Policies.
